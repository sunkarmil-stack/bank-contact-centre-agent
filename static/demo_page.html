    How approval works

    1. The AI can propose a card lock or a large dispute, but cannot carry it out alone.
    2. The call pauses and the action appears here with the reason it needs review.
    3. A reviewer approves or rejects. The person or system that requested the action cannot approve it.
    4. The approval is tied to this exact call and this exact action, so it cannot be reused or swapped.
    5. The paused call is saved securely, so it can resume on any server, even after a restart.

    Controls in this design

    ControlWhat it doesSupports
    Identity verification before disclosureAccount data is released only when the channel has verified the callerPrivacy safeguards, fraud prevention
    Purpose limitation and data minimisationEach data request declares its purpose; only the fields needed for that purpose are returnedPIPEDA principles
    Sensitive data redactionCard numbers, SIN, email, phone and similar are removed before the AI model, logs and memory see themPCI DSS scope reduction, privacy
    Four-eyes human reviewState-changing actions need approval from a different person, bound to the exact actionOperational risk, human oversight of AI
    Grounded, cited answersAnswers use approved knowledge only; citations are checked; otherwise the call goes to a personFair treatment, model risk (OSFI E-23)
    Manipulation screeningPrompt-injection attempts are blocked before reaching the model and filtered from retrieved contentTechnology and cyber risk (OSFI B-13)
    Complaint captureEvery expression of dissatisfaction becomes a case with a reference and timelineFCAC complaint-handling expectations
    Tamper-evident audit trailRecords are hash-chained so any edit or deletion is detectableAuditability, model validation evidence
